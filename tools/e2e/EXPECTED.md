# Expected placement per state (source of truth for the e2e matrix; re-read every round, the orchestrator may update it)

Universal invariants, every state and every transition:
- Duet's controls are fully visible, never overlap Safari's or the preview's own buttons, never sit under the status bar / camera / home indicator.
- Page content is never clipped or hidden under native chrome or under Duet's controls; no horizontal overflow (document scrollWidth <= innerWidth, except the documented left-column scroll offset).
- Transitions settle to the final placement within ~0.5s, with no slow drift, no flash in the wrong place.
- On a Duo in Safari, Duet's invisible edge guards cover the points Safari samples, 4px in from the left and right of the fixed-layout rect clipped to the document, at mid-height (README "Full-screen `position: fixed` overlay or modal backdrop", #147), in the final sample and through any stretch of 0.5s; a lag of a frame or two mid-turn is noted, and samples with the keyboard up are skipped (#151).

Per state (docs/how-duet-decides.md "Which mode" table is the spec):
- Closed, Safari tab, portrait: column, right.
- Closed, Safari tab, landscape angle 90: column, left. angle 270: column, right.
- Closed, in-app preview (SFSafariViewController sheet), all rotations: USER DECISION (2026-10-26): Duet's controls go INTO THE EMPTY GAP of the preview's own right column, between the preview's reader/menu button and its back button (in portrait roughly the band between the 2nd and 3rd native buttons, ~140pt tall on the real Duo; measure it per rotation). Compact controls (e.g. collapse to a single pill or the "…" menu plus selected tab) that fit the gap without touching any native button. The page keeps its full width (no inline strip), and nothing is clipped. Update the README "In-app preview" section and the spec accordingly. In rotations where no usable gap exists, fall back to the least intrusive option and document it.
- Landscape preview fallback (2026-10-26, round 2): the gap is ~26pt, too small. Least intrusive option chosen: controls hidden (data-hidden="preview"), page full width, preview's native buttons untouched. Harness expects `hidden` for preview-*-90/270, sheet and full screen alike (the full screen turned has no page column, its buttons in a safe-area inset).
- Landscape preview, round 4 (2026-10-27): the sheet keeps the hidden fallback. The default and full styles turned put every native button in a top bar and leave the 84pt safe-area inset on the turned side empty: controls go there as a compact strip (strip, left at 90, right at 270), below the top bar, page full width, no native overlap. This is the native gap of those styles, not an inline strip across the page.
- Unfolded preview (round 4): read as the preview (preview flag true). Sheet style: inline right like full width Safari. Default, full and over styles filling the open screen: wide, a strip in the 84pt right inset below the status column (clock, island, Live Activities); tall, inline right below the top bar and the island (matrix 2026-10-27, j26 to j36).
- Folding into closed landscape (2026-10-26, round 2, measured): Safari keeps its top bars (tab bar plus toolbar) in a 678x340 window with no column, the same window as a tab opened in landscape. No column exists to draw into, so Duet goes inline on the default side (right).
- Mid-turn and mid-fold: controls hidden (data-hidden="moving") with the previous placement kept; any visible sample at another placement is a flash and fails. This includes the closed portrait window an unfold from closed landscape passes through for ~90ms (fold-unfold-from-90b).
- Closed, preview -> "Open in Safari": becomes the tab placement above.
- Unfolded, full width: inline (user side, default right).
- Unfolded, split view, left pane and right pane: column (both edges drawn as documented); moving between panes and back to full width re-places correctly.
- Unfolded rotations (landscape both turns), split and full.
- Fold/unfold transitions between all of the above.

## Inspector

The inspector suites (`ins01`–`ins04`, `/lab/inspector?plain`) start with Household in the places capsule and no open inspector. Opening Rent folded gives it the full window and replaces places with the card commands. Each endpoint records `inspector.level` and `inspector.selected`; missing evidence fails the assertion. Closing restores Household and removes the card commands.

Unfolding preserves full screen and exposes Side by side. Tapping it gives the card a pane beside the overview, with Full screen as the toggle. Folding or entering a narrow split-view pane forces full screen; the same card remains selected through folding, turns 90/270, and left/right split. A grown window does not automatically undo full screen. Split must land in the requested native pane (the existing split driver checks this), or the step is BLOCKED.

Every eligible visible sample and recorded prepaint mutation must have the correct capsule owner: Household must not appear while a card is selected; card command labels must not appear after closing. Hidden/transit controls and background or rendering-held samples follow the existing exclusions. Folded commands are checked through the existing menu driver at the endpoint. The usual placement, clipping, native overlap, icon paint and settling checks still apply. Dark appearance and largest Dynamic Type repeat the same interaction expectations; they are simulator settings, not CSS viewport emulations.

## Home Screen web app (standalone)

The `sa*` suites (`--only standalone`, experimental) run the site as an installed Home Screen web app: the driver adds `SITE/` to the Home Screen through Safari's share sheet if no "Duet" clip exists, launches it from its icon, moves its page through the dev-only `/api/e2e-relay`, and removes the clip it added at teardown. Every sample must prove standalone from the page itself, `navigator.standalone` and `matchMedia("(display-mode: standalone)")` both true; a query param does not count.

Postures, on the lab (`sa01`–`sa04`, `/lab?tabs=5,searchTab=1,back=1,actions=2,title=1,feed=1,bar=1`) and on the inspector lab (`sa05`–`sa09`, `/lab/inspector?plain`): folded portrait, unfolded wide, unfolded with a card's pane open (inspector only, `sa07`), turn 90, turn 270 (pane open). In each, the column is docked: every group inside the window before the drags.

Two drags on the last group's last button, 1.5s each: across the page to 40pt from the far edge, and down (or up) the rail to 40pt from the far end. Through each drag the column stays in its rail on screen (page rects through the visual viewport, so a zoomed or panned page counts), with no flash elsewhere. Press feedback bound (Gabriele, 2026-11-22): each group may scale up to 1.13x its rest width and height, in place, its centre at most 1pt from its rest; anything past that is the column out of its rail. Inside the window. `press.ts`'s designed open (PRESS.width 78.8pt from a 49pt rest, about 1.6x, its pitch spread and spring overshoot) is past the bound and fails; the report keeps each drag's largest scale and drift (`press`). After each release the rects are identical to before and nothing keeps `--duet-drag` or `data-drag`. A drag whose touch the page did not see land on the column is BLOCKED.

Wealth landscape (`sa10`–`sa19`, `--only standalone-wealth`, experimental; Gabriele's report 2026-11-22: the prod wealth app on the Home Screen, turned to landscape, let its column be dragged out of its rail). A consumer app has no reporter or relay, so the driver starts `probe-proxy.mjs` in front of each build in `$E2E_WEALTH` (`label=origin,...`, local fixture servers, synthetic data only): one origin and one clip ("Wealth" or "Wealth management") for every build, the probe injected into its HTML, switching builds reloads the installed app. Per build, on `/`: `sa10` launched from the icon folded portrait, `sa11` turned 90, `sa12` turned 270; `sa13` unfolded wide, `sa14` turned 90, `sa15` turned 270; turned first, then launched from the icon: `sa16` folded 90, `sa17` folded 270, `sa18` unfolded 90, `sa19` unfolded 270. Each posture: docked before, then six drags, on the column's last button and from an empty point of the rail (the column's x, in the gap below its groups; skipped where that gap is under 60pt, and never above them, where the Duo's rail is the native status column and a drag opens Control Center without the page seeing it): sideways across the page, long along the rail, and a flick (160pt in 120ms). Same expectations as above through every drag: in its rail on screen within the 1.13x / 1pt bound, the same rects after each release, nothing left with `--duet-drag` or `data-drag`, every sample standalone. Wealth passes no `moveControls`, so no drag may switch its side. The report keeps the mode and side before and after, and the build each sample came from. A posture not reached, or a drag the page never saw, brings the app back once (Home, its icon, the fold again) and says so in the notes.

The iPad standalone counterpart is a Playwright WebKit spec (`e2e/standalone.spec.ts`), 1180x820 and 820x1180 with touch and `navigator.standalone` emulated: same docked and drag expectations on the site lab, the lab with `move=1`, and the inspector with a pane open.
